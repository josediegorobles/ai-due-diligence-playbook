# Contributing

Contributions are welcome if they make AI due diligence more practical, precise, and useful to senior practitioners.

## What Good Contributions Look Like

- concrete checklists
- diligence questions that expose real risk
- templates used in actual review work
- failure modes with mitigation guidance
- examples of evidence to request
- pragmatic regulatory readiness notes with sources
- security review items specific to AI systems

## What Does Not Belong

- generic AI ethics slogans
- vendor marketing
- benchmark worship without deployment context
- hype about AGI, disruption, or transformation
- academic literature summaries without operational use
- vague principles with no review action

## Style

- Be direct.
- Prefer checklists, tables, and decision criteria.
- Name red flags.
- Name blocking conditions.
- Ask for evidence.
- Assume the reader is busy and senior.
- Do not make legal claims without sources and caveats.

## Pull Request Checklist

- [ ] The contribution fits the repository scope.
- [ ] The content is practical and review-ready.
- [ ] Any regulatory claims include official or primary sources.
- [ ] The contribution avoids vendor-specific promotion.
- [ ] The contribution improves decision quality.
