# AI Due Diligence Playbook

Practical checklists, templates, and review frameworks for deciding whether an AI system is safe, secure, maintainable, compliant, commercially defensible, and ready for production.

This site is for Spanish SMEs, CTOs, founders, investors, engineering leaders, security teams, and diligence advisors who need evidence before approving, buying, deploying, or investing in AI systems.

Use the playbook when you need to answer one question:

> What evidence proves this AI system is ready for the risk it creates?

## Review Philosophy

Strong AI systems have boring evidence: owners, logs, evals, incident paths, rollback plans, vendor terms, data lineage, test sets, monitoring, and known limits.

Weak AI systems have screenshots, claims, demos, and vibes.
