# For Investors

AI claims can inflate a roadmap, a valuation, or an acquisition narrative. Diligence should separate durable technical advantage from demos, vendor wrappers, untested automation, and hidden compliance or security risk.

## Recommended Path

1. [AI Technical Due Diligence Report Template](../templates/ai-technical-due-diligence-report-template.md): structure the review and investment decision.
2. [AI Vendor Evaluation Template](../templates/ai-vendor-evaluation-template.md): request evidence for vendor, data, model, and operational claims.
3. [Acquisition Review of an LLM Support Tool](../case-studies/acquisition-review-llm-support-tool.md): see a fictional acquisition review pattern.
4. [AI Due Diligence Risk Model](../frameworks/ai-due-diligence-risk-model.md): score risk in a way that maps to deal terms and remediation.
5. [Example AI Risk Register](../examples/example-risk-register.md): track findings, severity, owner, and required actions.

## Deal Signals

| Signal | Diligence meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Proprietary data claim | Verify ownership, transferability, quality, consent, and actual model impact. |
| High automation claim | Check oversight, reversibility, incident handling, and liability exposure. |
| Model wrapper | Determine whether the value is product workflow, distribution, data, or just integration. |
| Enterprise readiness | Inspect retrieval permissions, tenant isolation, audit logs, security review, and SLAs. |
| AI Act exposure | Confirm classification, roles, evidence gaps, and contractual allocation of obligations. |
